Learning Science · Foundations 1 & 2

Why Struggle Is the Feature, Not the Bug

The Generation Effect — Slamecka & Graf

Self-generated information is retained significantly longer than passively received information.

"Even a wrong attempt beats reading the correct answer."

The attempt itself encodes context, prediction error, and schema — none of which happen when AI generates for you.

Desirable Difficulties — Robert Bjork

Conditions that slow initial learning produce stronger long-term retention.

  • 1 Spacing — spread practice over time
  • 2 Interleaving — mix problem types
  • 3 Retrieval practice — test, don't re-read

AI that removes friction removes the mechanism of learning itself.

The friction is not a bug in the learning process. It is the learning process.
Learning Science · Foundations 3 & 4

The Scaffold Must Fade

Zone of Proximal Development — Vygotsky
Can do alone
ZPD
with guidance
Out of reach

AI should stretch you into the ZPD — not skip over it by doing the work. The scaffold must fade as competence grows.

Expertise Reversal Effect — John Sweller

Scaffolding that helps novices actively harms experts.

As a developer's skill grows, step-by-step AI guidance becomes cognitive interference — filling working memory with redundant information, crowding out the expert's own efficient schemas.

Implication: The right level of AI help is not static. Sensei Mode must continuously calibrate — less scaffold as you grow, more only when genuinely stuck.

Learning Science · Foundation 5

Automation Complacency

Parasuraman's Research

When humans delegate to automation, they reduce monitoring, lose situational awareness, and suffer skill atrophy — even while feeling more capable.

✈️

Air France 447 — 2009

Pilots flew the Airbus A330 manually for so rarely that when automation failed at 38,000 ft, they could not recover basic manual flight. The autopilot had maintained their competence — until it couldn't.

228 lives. The cost of outsourced situational awareness.

SKILL LEVEL OVER TIME

The parallel to software development: when the AI always drives, the human loses the ability to steer — precisely when the AI makes its inevitable mistake.

Learning Science · Foundation 6

Bloom's 2 Sigma Problem

Benjamin Bloom — 1984

1:1 human tutoring produces a 2 standard deviation improvement in student outcomes — moving the average student to the 98th percentile.

The problem: human tutors don't scale. For decades, this was an unsolvable constraint. AI changes the equation.

Sensei Mode Design — Scaffolding Tiers

Five Levels of Guidance

5
Full Scaffold when stuck
"Here's the shape of what you need — try filling in the handler logic."
4
Guided Discovery
"What patterns do you know for decoupling?"
3
Socratic Questioning DEFAULT
"Why are you importing X directly? What happens if you add a third subscriber?"
2
Minimal Hint
"Think about coupling."
1
Observer Mode
Watches. Waits. Only speaks when asked or a critical mistake is imminent.

Escalation Logic

  • Start at Level 3 by default
  • Move to 4–5 only when developer signals genuine blockage
  • Move down to 1–2 as competence on this concept grows
  • Never skip levels up without justification

Vygotsky's ZPD: the scaffold at Level 4–5 is temporary — it exists to be removed. The goal is always to fade.

Anti-pattern to avoid:

Going straight to Level 5 because it's faster. This is exactly how full delegation starts.

Sensei Mode Design — Core Principle

The Lightest Touch Possible

💡 Hint "Think about coupling." One word, maximum leverage.
❓ Question "What happens if you add a third subscriber?"
📖 Explanation Concept clarification without implementation.
🔍 Example Analogous pattern from a different domain.
💻 Code Last resort. Only when all else fails. Partial preferred.

The Pair Programming Escalation Model

Best pair programmers default to hints. They escalate to direct instruction only when their partner is genuinely blocked — and even then, they prefer to guide rather than take over the keyboard.

Each step to the right is a step toward dependency. Use the lightest intervention that unblocks forward motion.
